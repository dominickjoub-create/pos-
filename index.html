The Shop

A cut above, the old-fashioned way

Nunus Barber Shop is Krugersdorp's home of precise, unhurried grooming. Set inside Heritage Square, it's a warm room of exposed brick, reclaimed walnut and polished chrome — the kind of place where a haircut is a ritual, not a transaction.

At the chair you'll find Hemza, a long-trusted barber whose steady hand has shaped generations of the same families. Kids who took their first cut here now bring their own. That's the trust we've built — clean, precise, welcoming, every single time.

  • Generational loyalty & genuine care
  • Meticulous fades, tapers & classic scissor work
  • Spotless, well-organised, relaxed atmosphere
